How to play

The classic three-in-a-row game on a 3 by 3 grid. Line up three of your marks before your opponent does.

  1. Choose vs Computer or 2 Players.
  2. On your turn, click an empty square to place your mark.
  3. Line up three marks in a row, a column or a diagonal to win.
  4. If every square fills with no line, the round is a draw.
  5. The difficulty bar changes how hard the computer plays. Unbeatable cannot be beaten.
  6. New round clears the board; Reset score clears the tally.
